# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PRAABINDH CORP and the PADS maintainers are committed to making participation in
this project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports the community include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, experiences, and communication needs;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing critique on work and user outcomes rather than people;
- taking responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from the
  experience;
- prioritizing the community's accessibility, safety, and shared goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, or attention;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- deliberate exclusion, intimidation, or disruption;
- abuse of issue reports, reviews, or security channels;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public or private spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private
contact method listed on the
[`@praabindh`](https://github.com/praabindh) GitHub profile. Do not include
sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy as far as
practical, and respond in a manner proportionate to the circumstances. Possible
responses include correction, warning, temporary restriction, permanent removal,
and escalation to the relevant platform. Maintainers who have a conflict of
interest must recuse themselves from the case.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this policy.
